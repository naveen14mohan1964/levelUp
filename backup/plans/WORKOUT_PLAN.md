# Home Workout & Fat-Loss Plan: 115 kg → 80 kg

**Profile:** 23 · 185 cm · 115 kg (BMI 33.6) · sedentary desk job · non-vegetarian · no injuries reported · bodyweight now, resistance bands from Week 4
**Schedule:** 35-min workout at 5:35 AM (before the DSA morning block) · 7h sleep (10:30 PM → 5:30 AM)
**Runs alongside:** the 22-week DSA plan, Mon Oct 5, 2026 → Sun Mar 7, 2027

> ⚕️ **See a doctor before you start.** At a BMI over 30, coming from a sedentary routine, get a general check-up first. Ask for a **BP reading, HbA1c / fasting glucose, lipid profile, and thyroid (TSH)**. It's cheap and quick, it rules out hidden issues, and it gives you a baseline that will look great to compare against in 6 months.
> **Stop immediately and get checked** if you have chest pain or tightness, dizziness, unusual breathlessness, or sharp joint pain (as opposed to normal muscle burn).

## 1. The honest timeline

A safe pace is **0.5–1 kg/week**. You'll lose faster in the first month (partly water), then it slows as your weight drops. **80 kg is a 13–15 month journey, not 5.** In this 5-month plan, a realistic result is **100–103 kg (−12 to −15 kg)**, plus a big drop in waist size and a huge gain in fitness.

| Milestone | Badge | Realistic window | Approx. date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥉 110 kg (−5) | *First Five* | Weeks 5–7 | mid–late Nov 2026 |
| 🥈 105 kg (−10) | *Double Digits* | Weeks 12–14 | late Dec 2026 – early Jan 2027 |
| 🥇 100 kg (−15) | *Two-Digit Club* | Weeks 20–24 | late Feb – mid Mar 2027 ← **5-month target** |
| 95 kg (−20) | *Halfway Hero* (57%) | ~Month 7 | ~May 2027 |
| 90 kg (−25) | *Quarter Century* | ~Month 9 | ~Jul 2027 |
| 85 kg (−30) | *Almost There* | ~Month 11 | ~Sep 2027 |
| 🏆 80 kg (−35) | *Goal Crusher* | Months 13–15 | Nov 2027 – Jan 2028 |

**Non-scale wins to track too:** waist −8 to −12 cm by March · going from wall push-ups to floor push-ups · a 45-minute walk without getting winded · better sleep and energy during DSA blocks.

**Weight fluctuates by 0.5–1.5 kg day to day** because of water, salt and food volume. Weigh yourself every morning (after the toilet, before eating) and **only judge progress by the 7-day average**. The app will chart this for you.

## 2. Daily targets

| Target | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Calories** | **~2,000–2,100 kcal** | Your estimated maintenance (sedentary) is ~2,600 kcal, so this is a ~500–600 kcal deficit. *This estimate assumes male physiology. If that's wrong, tell me and I'll recalculate (it would be ~1,850).* |
| **Protein** | **~130 g/day** (about 30–35 g per meal) | Protects muscle while you lose fat and keeps you full. |
| **Steps** | **5,000** in Weeks 1–2, then **+1,000 every 2 weeks** → **10,000 by Week 11**, then hold | Office walks every 2 hours, phone calls on foot, a 10-min walk after dinner. |
| **Water** | **3–3.5 L** (12–14 glasses) | A glass on waking, one before each meal, and a bottle at your desk. |
| **Sleep** | **7 hours** (10:30 PM → 5:30 AM) | Poor sleep raises hunger and cravings, and kills willpower for both DSA and diet. |
| **Vegetables** | Half your plate at lunch and dinner | Volume + fibre = full on fewer calories. |

**Calorie adjustment rule** (use the 7-day average weight):
- Losing **less than 0.4 kg/week for 2 weeks in a row:** cut 150 kcal (never go below 1,700 without a doctor's advice).
- Losing **more than 1.2 kg/week after Month 1:** add 150 kcal. That pace is too fast and costs muscle.
- **Every 5 kg lost:** reduce the target by ~70 kcal, since a smaller body burns less.

## 3. Nutrition: cooking for yourself (non-veg)

**Plate method** (no weighing needed after the first month): ½ vegetables/salad · ¼ protein (palm-sized, ~150 g cooked chicken/fish, or 3–4 eggs) · ¼ carbs (1 cup cooked rice or 2 rotis) · plus curd or buttermilk.

**Sample day (~2,000 kcal, ~130 g protein):**

| Meal | Example | Protein | kcal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Breakfast, ~8:00 (after workout) | 3-egg + 2-white omelette with onion/tomato/spinach + 2 multigrain rotis or 2 slices of brown bread | ~35 g | ~450 |
| Lunch, office (carried from home) | 150 g chicken or fish curry (1 tsp oil) + 1 cup rice or 2 rotis + dal + salad + curd | ~40 g | ~650 |
| Snack, ~4:30 PM | 40 g roasted chana + buttermilk, or a fruit + 1 boiled egg | ~15 g | ~250 |
| Dinner, ~7:00 PM | 150 g grilled/tandoori chicken (or paneer bhurji) + a big bowl of sautéed vegetables + 1 roti | ~40 g | ~600 |

**High-protein foods to rotate:** eggs (6 g each) · chicken breast (31 g/100 g) · fish (20–25 g/100 g) · curd/Greek yogurt (5–10 g/100 g) · paneer (18 g/100 g) · dal and chana (7–9 g per cooked cup) · soya chunks (~50 g dry per 100 g). Whey protein is optional, not required.

**Cook-ahead (so cooking doesn't eat into DSA time):**
- **Sunday, 1 hour:** boil 10 eggs · marinate 1 kg chicken and bake/air-fry/pan-grill it, then portion into 150 g boxes · cook dal for 3 days · chop vegetables for 3 days
- **Wednesday, 30 min:** a second batch of chicken/dal + vegetables
- **Oil:** measure it. 1 tsp per person per dish, 3–4 tsp per day in total. Oil is the #1 hidden calorie source in Indian cooking.

**Ordering-out rules (for when you do):** pick tandoori/grilled dishes over gravies and fried food · eat half the biryani and add a raita · no sugary drinks · skip the extra naan.
**Sugar in tea/coffee:** cut it by half every week until it's ≤ 1 tsp per cup.
**80/20 rule:** one relaxed meal a week (not a "cheat day"). There's no guilt and no "making up for it" the next day.

## 4. Weekly structure (35 minutes each)

| Day | Session | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | 💪 Strength A | 5-min warm-up → 25 min strength → 5-min cool-down |
| **Tue** | 🚶 Cardio + core | Warm-up → 20–30 min low-impact intervals → core finisher |
| **Wed** | 💪 Strength B | Warm-up → strength → cool-down |
| **Thu** | 🚶 Cardio + mobility | Warm-up → low-impact intervals → 10-min mobility flow |
| **Fri** | 💪 Strength A (odd weeks) / B (even weeks) | Warm-up → strength → cool-down |
| **Sat** | 🌳 Long walk | 35 → 50 min brisk walk outdoors (or stairs/indoor walking) |
| **Sun** | 😌 Rest | Weigh-in, measurements, photos (see §7). An easy walk is optional. |

**Why low-impact:** at 115 kg, every jump puts roughly 3–5× your body weight through your knees and ankles. Marching, stepping, boxing moves and walking burn plenty of calories without that strain. Jumping is optional, and only from Phase 5 if your knees feel completely fine.

**Effort guide:**
- **Strength:** end each set with **2–3 good reps still left in the tank**. You should never grind to failure.
- **Cardio:** use the **talk test**. You should be able to speak in sentences, but not sing.

### Standard warm-up (5 min), same every day
- [ ] March in place, 60 s (*swing your arms, stand tall*)
- [ ] Arm circles, 15 s forward + 15 s back
- [ ] Hip circles, 10 each way (*hands on hips, slow*)
- [ ] Bodyweight good-mornings ×10 (*soft knees, push your hips back, flat back*)
- [ ] Sit-to-stand from a chair ×8 (*slow, controlled*)
- [ ] Cat–cow ×6 (*move one vertebra at a time*)

### Standard cool-down (5 min)
- [ ] Standing quad stretch, 30 s/side (*hold a wall, knees together*)
- [ ] Hamstring stretch (heel on a low step), 30 s/side (*hinge from the hips, flat back*)
- [ ] Kneeling hip-flexor stretch, 30 s/side (*squeeze the glute of the back leg; this undoes desk sitting*)
- [ ] Doorway chest stretch, 30 s (*elbows at shoulder height*)
- [ ] Child's pose, 45 s (*breathe slowly through the nose*)

## 5. The program: 5 phases, progressing every 3–4 weeks

**Progression rule (double progression):** each exercise has a rep range. When you hit the **top of the range on every set with clean form for 2 sessions in a row**, progress to the harder variation, slow the tempo, add band tension, or add weight to a backpack. On top of that, the whole program steps up at each phase change.

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3 · Oct 5–25)
*Goal: learn the movements, build the habit, protect your joints. It should feel easy, and that's the point.*

**Strength A** (rest 60 s between sets)

|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Chair sit-to-stand | 2 × 10 | 60 s | Hips back first, knees track over your toes, stand up by driving through your heels |
| [ ] | Wall push-up | 2 × 10–12 | 60 s | Body in one straight line from head to heels; lower your chest to the wall slowly |
| [ ] | Glute bridge | 2 × 12 | 45 s | Squeeze your glutes at the top for 1 s; keep your ribs down so your lower back doesn't arch |
| [ ] | Bird dog | 2 × 6/side | 45 s | Move slowly, keeping your hips level as if there's a cup of tea on your back |
| [ ] | Standing calf raise (hold a wall) | 2 × 15 | 45 s | Pause 1 s at the top and lower slowly |
| [ ] | Dead bug | 2 × 6/side | 45 s | Press your lower back into the floor the whole time |

**Strength B**
| ✓ | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest | Form tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Wall sit | 2 × 20–30 s | 60 s | Back flat on the wall; go only as low as is comfortable |
| [ ] | Incline push-up (kitchen counter) | 2 × 8 | 60 s | Hands slightly wider than shoulders, elbows at 45°, not flared out |
| [ ] | Supported reverse lunge (hold a chair) | 2 × 6/leg | 60 s | Step back, then lower straight down; front knee over the middle of your foot |
| [ ] | Hip hinge (bodyweight good-morning) | 2 × 12 | 45 s | Push your hips back like closing a car door with your butt; flat back |

## 6. Low-energy, missed-day & pain rules

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Tired / bad sleep / late night** | **"10-minute mode"** (it still counts for your streak): 2 rounds of 10 sit-to-stands + 10 wall/incline push-ups + 10 glute bridges + 30 s march. Or just a 10-min walk. |
| **Missed a workout** | Don't double up. Do the next scheduled session and carry on. |
| **Missed a whole week** | Repeat the previous week before moving on. |
| **Muscle soreness (DOMS), 24–72 h** | Normal in the first weeks and after phase changes. Light movement helps more than rest. |
| **Sharp or joint pain** | Stop that exercise and use the easier variation from the previous phase. If it lasts more than a week, see a doctor or physio. |
| **Sick (fever, chest)** | Rest completely. When you return, use the previous phase for 1 week. |

## Jogging (walk-jog progression)
Added 2026-10-13. Easy, conversational jogging in short intervals that grow each phase, so knees, shins and tendons adapt at 110+ kg.
Tuesday is **Jog + core** from week 1; Thursday becomes **Jog + mobility** from week 4 (weeks 1–3 keep low-impact cardio).
Every session: 5 min brisk walk to warm up, the intervals, 5 min walk to cool down.

| Weeks | Intervals |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 6 × (30 s easy jog + 2 min walk) |
| 4–7 | 6 × (1 min jog + 2 min walk) |
| 8–11 | 5 × (2 min jog + 2 min walk) |
| 12 (deload) | 6 × (1 min jog + 2 min walk) |
| 13–16 | 4 × (4 min jog + 2 min walk) |
| 17 (deload) | 5 × (2 min jog + 2 min walk) |
| 18–22 | 3 × (8 min jog + 2 min walk) |

Rules: jog slowly enough to talk in full sentences; short, quick steps landing under your hips; good shoes, flat or soft ground.
Stop on sharp knee, shin or chest pain, and repeat a stage if it felt hard. If you have a heart or joint condition, check with a doctor first.
